Race day, sorted.

Lane Judge is an iPhone app for gravity car races: scout packs, church clubs, classrooms and kitchen floors.

Get Lane Judge on the App Store

Heat chart in one tap. Every car runs once in every lane, on tracks with 2 to 6 lanes.
Tap to score. Tap the cars in the order they finish. Standings update after every heat.
Finish line camera. Hold the iPhone over the line; it reports the order and the gaps in milliseconds.
Finals and PDF. A finals round for the fastest cars, and results to print for the pack.

Help

The camera calls a finish before the cars arrive. Something moved on the line: a hand, a shadow or the phone itself. Prop the phone on something solid and keep the line clear after tapping Arm.

The camera misses a car. Add light, and place the line just past the point where the cars arrive so each car fully covers it. Very dark tracks with dark cars are the hardest case.

A result is wrong. Open the heat and tap the cars in the right order. The standings follow.
