Lane Judge

Privacy policy

In force from 19 November 2026.

Races and names

The races you create, the car numbers and the names you type are saved only inside the app on your iPhone. There is no account and no server: the developer never receives them. Deleting a race removes it; deleting the app removes everything.

Camera

The finish line camera is used only while the judging screen is open. The app reads the brightness of the pixels under the line you draw to notice a car crossing. It does not save photos or video, does not use the microphone and sends nothing anywhere.

Purchases

Lane Judge Pro is bought through Apple's App Store, and Apple handles the payment. So the developer can count sales, the app reports each purchase (which product, when, price, country and a random identifier made by the app) to RevenueCat, a purchase analytics service. That record carries no name, no email and nothing about your races, and it is not used to follow you in other apps or sites.

Children

Lane Judge is a tool for the adults who run a race. It has no ads, no chat, no links for children to follow, and it collects nothing about the racers beyond the names an adult types, which stay on the device.
